Minutes of Meeting

Unique Ambassadors — Funeral Planning Committee

Monday, 8 August 2026, 9:00 PM (GMT)

Attendance President, Brother Jaka Jusu Ngobeh•Secretary General, Brother Fomba Kassoh•Brother Salim Massaquio•Dr. Kenneh•Chairman Kekeh Sahr Lamin•Brother Blyden•Brother Kemoh•Brother Patrice•Dr. Mohamed•Brother Idriss Lamin•Brother Yokorbor•Brother Abdulai (joined, then called out sick)•Brother Gadiru (joined, left early)•Brother Lahai Amara (joined, left early)

Opening

  • The meeting opened with 30 seconds of silent prayer.
  • The President, Brother Jaka Jusu Ngobeh, gave opening remarks, thanking the brothers who have already contributed and urging those still outstanding to complete their pledges so the group can move forward.

Decisions Made

  • Total pledges are expected to reach around Le73,000; available funds are sufficient to settle Dr. Kenneh's food/catering request of about Le39,000.
  • Chairman Kekeh Sahr Lamin's pledge was revised from the recorded Le10,000 to his intended Le3,000, and has been fully honored.
  • Proceed with one cow for now; a second will only be considered if funds remain after all other essential items are covered.
  • Banners confirmed at seven in total — two for up-country and five for vehicles in Kailahun, pending final confirmation.
  • A new mini-bus has been secured for the trip, available for collection on Thursday, with a driver already arranged.
  • The souvenir/order-of-service booklet will be kept simple and cost-effective — a modest number of hard copies for the church, plus an electronic (PDF) version shared on the forum for those without a hard copy. Target: finalized by Wednesday.
  • The booklet will not carry lengthy individual tributes — maybe only three brief statements: one from Unique Ambassadors, one from the family, and one from the school.
  • The match pass will proceed with the coffin from the school to the church, then from the church to Torgbombu cemetery, as requested by Methodist Secondary School and the old boys.
  • Students will assemble at the school by 8:00 a.m., with the procession departing for the church by approximately 9:30 a.m.; laying-out will take place at the school, and possibly also at the church.
  • Prior commitments (food, transportation, and other essentials already promised) will be honored regardless of the funding shortfall.

Outstanding Issues

  • Full costing for stickers and pinups not yet finalized (banner count is confirmed; pricing across all three items still pending).
  • Discrepancy between individual payment records and the treasurer's report needs reconciliation.
  • There may be a funding shortfall against the full remaining budget; brothers may need to contribute again.
  • Availability and cost of a marching band not yet confirmed — Methodist Secondary School no longer has its own band; status of the National Secondary School's band still unknown.
  • Whether a private/outside band will be needed, and at what cost, still to be decided; brothers may consider hiring bands from out of town.
  • Departure time, pickup locations, and full transportation itinerary for travelers from Freetown not yet finalized.

Tasks Assigned

  • Treasurer, Brother Lahai Amara, and Financial Secretary, Brother Lahai Gbessay — reconcile payment records and confirm accurate outstanding balances.
  • Brother Salim Massaquio — finalize and share costing for stickers and pinups via the forum; share photos/materials for the booklet with Brother Fomba Kassoh.
  • Secretary General, Brother Fomba Kassoh — reorganize and finalize the order-of-service booklet using materials received from Brother Salim Massaquio and Dr. Kenneh.
  • EPD — report back on the availability and cost of school marching bands (Methodist and National); also provide cost and availability of a private band.
  • President, Brother Jaka Jusu Ngobeh — follow up directly with the relevant band contact if needed.
  • Dr. Kenneh — report with departure time, pickup locations, and full transportation itinerary details after the family meeting tomorrow.
  • All brothers — review prior assignments and come prepared with updates at the general meeting; be ready to contribute further if a shortfall remains.

Motion to Adjourn

  • A motion was made to adjourn the meeting until the general meeting, moved by the Secretary General, Brother Fomba Kassoh, and seconded by Dr. Kenneh.

Next Meeting: General meeting, 9 August 2026 at 9:00 p.m.
