Minutes of Meeting

Unique Ambassadors — Funeral Planning Committee

In memory of Ansinu Keleti Lamin (AKL)

Opening

  • The meeting opened with a minute of silent prayer for AKL.
  • President Jaka Jusu Ngobeh gave opening remarks, thanking members for their continued effort toward a befitting funeral for AKL, and outlined the purpose of the meeting: to review contributions and expectations.

Finance Report

  • Total pledges were reported at approximately Le67,500; about Le20,452 had been collected so far.
  • Outstanding individual balances were not yet available. Treasurer Lahai Amara and Financial Secretary Lahai Gbessay were asked to compile the full list of pledges, collections, and expenditures and report back the following morning.
  • Approximately Le11,000+ had been spent on the grave, and Le400–500 on the banner, so far.
  • A key question was raised: once outstanding pledges are collected, will the committee be able to cover the Le38,000 catering bill and a vehicle purchase? This was deferred pending the full financial reconciliation.

T-Shirts & Attire

  • Salim reported that the printer has about 25 sample copies ready in black and white; the final design and colour will be confirmed after further consultation with the printer.
  • Cost is around Le100 per unit, in line with the earlier quote.
  • About five contributors have paid so far; members were encouraged to pay promptly, as the cost of the T-shirts is significant.
  • Wives of members are also entitled to shirts.

Caps

  • Members must indicate on the Unique Ambassadors WhatsApp forum if they want a cap, including replacements for lost or damaged ones.
  • A new, improved cap design is being considered. Members were encouraged to purchase the new one for uniformity, even those who already have an old one.
  • Caps are expected to be ready by Wednesday or Thursday, contingent on payments being made on time.

Banners

  • Two banners have been received: one placed at the entrance of Methodist Secondary School, Kailahun, and one at the residence.
  • The committee agreed not to add the school badge to the existing banners. The school may print their own banner.

Order of Service Booklet

  • A quantity of around 400–500 copies was proposed for the church service, with photo pages printed in colour and text pages in black and white.
  • Salim will obtain samples and costing from the printer and report back so the committee can settle on a final quantity and price.

Pins / Program Buttons

  • The committee agreed these are needed. Quantity was estimated between 600 and 1,000, with 1,000 favoured.
  • Cost is to be sourced before the final quantity is confirmed.

Stickers

  • To be discussed further; intended for the vehicles taking part in the procession.

Procession / Match Pass

  • The committee agreed that no procession/match pass is required from the school to the church.
  • A formal procession will take place only from the church to Torgbombu cemetery, accompanying the coffin.
  • Viewing and laying-out at the school will not be compulsory for students.
  • The committee will invite the marching bands of both Methodist Secondary School and the National Secondary School. It was noted that an invitation does not necessarily mean the band performs free of charge — terms are to be confirmed, and payment arranged in advance if required.

Tributes

  • Four tribute slots are planned, in order: family, place of work, church, and friends.
  • Salim Massaquio was assigned responsibility for coordinating the "friends" tribute, drawing on contacts among ambassadors and from grade school and secondary school.

Travel / Itinerary — Unique Ambassadors Brothers

  • The memorial service is expected on Sunday; church attendance was confirmed as important for all Unique Ambassadors brothers.
  • After discussion, it was agreed that the brothers will depart Kailahun for Freetown on Monday morning at 10:00 a.m.

Next Meeting

  • The committee agreed to meet again on Monday evening at 9:00 p.m., ahead of the general meeting on Tuesday, to finalize outstanding financial and logistical items.
  • Secretary General Fomba Kassoh will continue preparing the minutes of the meetings.

Meeting closed with a motion to adjourn until Monday, 9:00 p.m., duly seconded and approved.

In the soul of our brother, rest in perfect peace. Amen.
